## 6. 如何迁移到 Obsidian Vault

打开 PowerShell，执行：

```powershell
# 复制到 Obsidian Vault
$src = "G:\15389\Claude Skills 知识库"
$dst = "G:\Obsidian Vault\Knowledge\Claude Skills 知识库"

Write-Host "Done! Copied to $dst"
```
